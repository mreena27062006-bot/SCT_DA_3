# IBM HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Task Overview
**Task 03 — Interactive Visualization | SkillCraft Technology Internship**

Built an interactive HR analytics dashboard using Tableau Public on the 
IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset to answer:
> *"Why are employees leaving?"*

---

## 🔗 Live Dashboard
👉 https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/mneddula.reena/viz/IBMHRAttritionAnalyticsDashboard_17794517359880/IBMHRAttritionDashboard?publish=yes
---

## 📁 Dataset
- **Source:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset
- **Platform:** [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset)
- **Records:** 1,470 employees
- **Key Columns:** Attrition, Department, Age, JobSatisfaction, 
  MonthlyIncome, OverTime, YearsAtCompany, Gender

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

| Visual | Chart Type | Insight |
|---|---|---|
| KPI Cards | Text | Total Employees, Attrition Count, Rate %, Avg Years |
| Attrition by Department | Pie Chart | Which dept loses most people |
| Attrition by Age Group | Treemap | Which age group leaves most |
| Attrition by Gender | Stacked Bar | Gender-wise attrition split |
| Job Satisfaction by Role | Bar Chart | Low satisfaction roles |
| Overtime vs Attrition | Stacked Bar | Overtime impact on attrition |

**Interactive Filters:** Department · Age Group · OverTime

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. **Sales department** has the highest attrition — 92 out of 446 employees
2. **Age group 26–35** records the highest attrition volume (41 employees)
3. **Overtime employees** show significantly higher attrition (48 Yes vs 44 No among OT workers)
4. **Sales Representatives** have the lowest average job satisfaction score (2.27)
5. **Overall attrition rate** is 16.12% across 1,470 employees
